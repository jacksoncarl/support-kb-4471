The two problems

  • Parser reads day-first dates month first. A day-first tenant’s dates come out flipped. This is what #4471 describes, and the queued fix is for this one.
  • booking-v3 hints MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants. The form’s hint tells people to type the month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way. The parser is not involved, so fixing the parser leaves these bookings wrong.
Do not close the second ticket when the parser fix ships. Check it against reports from the booking-v3 form after the fix.

How to tell them apart

Use the tenant and the channel: src/lib/kb/causes.ts decides. A report through the booking form of a tenant on booking-v3 is the hint problem. Every other report from a day-first tenant is the parser problem.

Do not use these three things:

  • The subject line. Decision 0001 merges on it, and both problems share the same subjects.
  • The intake tag. It is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant in both groups.
  • The raw row count. The export repeats some rows, so count each report id once.

The note under the table counts reports that read as a move of one day, not a flipped date. They are counted where the tenant files them. They do not have a ticket of their own yet, and the first ticket lists them to re-check after the parser fix.
